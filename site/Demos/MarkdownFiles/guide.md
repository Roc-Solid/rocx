### Before you go

Check the tide table: the lower path floods at high water.

#### What to bring

Binoculars, a notebook, and a pencil, which writes in the rain.

### On the path

Walk slowly and stop often. Most birds hear you before you see them, so
stand still for a minute at each bend and let them come back.

### Afterwards

Write down what you saw while you remember it: the time, the place, and
how many.
