# qr: proposals

What the package doesn't do yet, each with what it would take.

1. **Numeric and alphanumeric modes.** Digits alone take 3.3 bits a character in numeric mode, and
   upper-case letters, digits and ` $%*+-./:` take 5.5 in alphanumeric, against 8 in byte mode: an
   upper-case URL (`HTTPS://EXAMPLE.COM/X`) fits a smaller code. A segment of each, chosen by what
   the text is (mixed segments: the optimal split is a small dynamic programme).
2. **A higher level for free.** When the version a text needs at one level also holds it at a higher
   one, use the higher (Nayuki's `boostEcl`): the same size, more damage it survives.
3. **A logo in the middle.** At `High`, a small image over the centre still reads. The component
   would take a child to put there, and say how much of the code it may cover.
4. **ECI and Kanji modes, structured append, micro QR codes**: none asked for.
